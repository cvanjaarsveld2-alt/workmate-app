## Where the numbers come from

Most "after" figures can be read straight from PowerMate:

- **Days to invoice:** job completion date vs. invoice date (Reports / Job profit, CSV export).
- **Quotes sent and won:** Quotes screen and the dashboard's sales pipeline.
- **Days until paid:** invoice date vs. payment date (Invoices, "Export for accounting").
- **Job profit:** Job profit screen for the period.

"Before" figures come from their old spreadsheets, their accounting system, or the owner's
best estimate (say which).
