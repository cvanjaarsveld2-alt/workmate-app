## 1. Who we are

[Operator legal name] (registration number [number]) provides the App. Our Information Officer is [name], [email], [phone]. This policy explains how we handle personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

## 2. Two roles

- **For the people who use the App** (a company's staff), we are the *responsible party* for their account information.
- **For the information a company loads about its own clients and contacts**, the company is the responsible party and we are its *operator*, acting only on its instructions (see the Data Processing Agreement).

## 3. What we collect about Users

- Account details: name, email address, company, role.
- Security details: sign-in times, device type, two-step login status. PINs are stored only on the device, as a salted hash.
- Usage and error information: which screens fail and when, to fix problems.
- Terms acceptance: version and date.

## 4. Why we use it

To provide and secure the App, support Users, prevent fraud and abuse, bill the Customer, meet legal obligations and improve the App. We do not sell personal information and do not use Customer Data for advertising.

## 5. Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting providers ([Supabase — region], [Vercel]). Some providers may process data outside South Africa; where they do, we rely on POPIA section 72 safeguards (adequate protection laws or binding agreements).

## 6. How long we keep it

Account information is kept while the account is active and for [12] months afterwards, or longer where the law requires (for example tax records). Customer Data is deleted [30] days after a Customer leaves, unless the Customer asks otherwise or the law requires us to keep it.

## 7. Security

Encryption in transit, per-company access controls enforced by the database, optional two-step login, device PIN lock, least-privilege staff access and regular backups. We notify affected Customers and the Information Regulator of security compromises as POPIA section 22 requires.

## 8. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, object to processing, or complain to the Information Regulator (inforeg.org.za, complaints.IR@justice.gov.za). Requests about a company's client data should go to that company first; we will help it respond.
