## 1. Parties and roles

The Customer is the *responsible party* for personal information it loads into the App (its clients, contacts, staff and suppliers). [Operator legal name] is its *operator* under POPIA sections 20 and 21.

## 2. Instructions

We process Customer Data only to provide the App and as the Customer instructs through its settings and requests. We will tell the Customer if we believe an instruction breaks POPIA.

## 3. Confidentiality

Only staff who need access to support or run the App may access Customer Data. They are bound by confidentiality.

## 4. Security measures

- Each company's data is separated by database access rules that are tested automatically.
- Encryption in transit; private file storage with short-lived links.
- Optional two-step login; device PIN lock; audit log of key changes.
- Backups and a tested restore procedure.

## 5. Sub-operators

We use: [Supabase — database, authentication and file storage — region], [Vercel — web hosting], [email provider]. We will give [30] days' notice of new sub-operators. The Customer may object on reasonable grounds.

## 6. Security compromises

We will notify the Customer without undue delay, and within [72] hours of becoming aware, of any compromise affecting its data. We will give the information the Customer needs to notify the Information Regulator and data subjects.

## 7. Data subject requests

We will help the Customer respond to requests to access, correct or delete personal information, including through the App's export feature.

## 8. Return and deletion

The Customer may export its data at any time. When the agreement ends we delete Customer Data within [30] days of the Customer's request or of termination, except where the law requires otherwise.

## 9. Audits

On reasonable notice, and at most once a year, we will provide information reasonably needed to show compliance with this agreement.
